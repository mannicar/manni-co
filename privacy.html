Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Mannicode LLC ("Mannicode", "we", "us") collects and uses information through this website and our products, including Blu-cru.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide, such as your name, email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Work assignment and scheduling information entered into Blu-cru by you or your employer.
  • Basic technical information, such as device and browser type, used to operate and secure our services.

How we use information

  • To provide our products, including sending assignment notifications, schedule changes, shift reminders, and verification codes.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve our services.

Text messaging

If you opt in to Blu-cru text messages, we use your mobile number only to send the messages you agreed to receive. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share mobile numbers only with service providers that deliver messages on our behalf, such as our SMS provider.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our services, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us.
