Mannicode Blu-cru Privacy Terms

Blu-cru

Blu-cru is a workforce assignment product built and operated by Mannicode LLC. Managers use Blu-cru to assign workers to shifts and jobs, and workers get notified when they are assigned, when a schedule changes, and before a shift starts.

Text message alerts

Blu-cru can send workers SMS notifications about their assignments, including:

  • New assignment and shift notifications
  • Schedule changes and cancellations
  • Shift reminders
  • Account verification codes

Blu-cru does not send marketing or promotional text messages.

How workers opt in

Workers choose to receive text messages when they create or update their Blu-cru account by entering their mobile number and checking a consent box, which is unchecked by default. Opting in is not required to use Blu-cru. The consent box reads:

I agree to receive text messages from Blu-cru (operated by Mannicode LLC) about my assignments, schedule changes, shift reminders, and account verification at the number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any Blu-cru message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at support@manni.co.
